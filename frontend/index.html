Frequently Asked Questions

What is a bookmarklet and how does it work?

A bookmarklet is a simple web bookmark containing JavaScript. Instead of opening a website, clicking it injects mockTrader directly into your current tab so you can paper trade on any live chart without installing full browser extensions.

Do I need to connect my Web3 wallet or risk real crypto?

No! mockTrader uses simulated virtual funds (like paper SOL). You don't need to connect your wallet, approve any smart contracts, or deposit real funds.

Which trading platforms are supported?

mockTrader works smoothly on popular trading platforms including Axiom, GMGN, Padre, DexScreener, and TradingView chart overlays.

How do I show my bookmarks bar?

In Chrome/Brave/Edge, press Ctrl + Shift + B (or Cmd + Shift + B on Mac) to toggle your bookmarks bar visible, then drag the button up into it.
